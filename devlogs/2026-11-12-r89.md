# Devlog, 2026-11-12: build r89

## Lock-on by touch

Lock-on used to sit on D-pad Up. It is now a **tap on the front screen**: tap to lock onto the enemy nearest the centre of view, tap again to release, flick the right stick to move the lock to the next enemy on that side. Taps on open menus, dialogs and the top-right menu corner work as before.

## Debug Start

The title menu has a third option next to Continue and New Game. **Debug Start** puts you by Firelink Shrine's bonfire as a Knight with every weapon, armour piece, ring and spell the port can show, a stack of consumables, 200,000 souls, 10 humanity, Estus at 10 and every bonfire lit. It makes testing any part of the game a two-minute job instead of an hour of playing there.

## Frame pacing

The Vita logs showed the game swinging between 25 and 43 fps under 60 Hz vsync, which looks jittery even when the average is fine. The engine now **locks to 30 fps** (every second vblank), so every frame is on screen for the same time. In Vita3K it holds 30 with no late frames. The frame profile also gained the HUD and the unmeasured remainder of each frame, the first step towards finding where the rest of the Vita's frame time goes.

## PS5 controller in Vita3K

For demos on the PC: in Vita3K's PS TV mode the game now reads the controller's extra buttons. L1/R1 guard and attack, L2/R2 parry and strong-attack, R3 locks on. The buttons work. The pad arrives on port 1 in that mode and port 0 reports all-zero sticks. The sticks still need one more fix: the extra ports' analog sampling is now switched on, and that build is waiting on a test.

## Also in this build

- Event scripts run on a time base (30 Hz) instead of every second frame.
- A new game that wasn't saved no longer loads an older save over itself.
